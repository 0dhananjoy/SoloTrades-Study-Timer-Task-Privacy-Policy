Privacy Policy

Last updated & effective date: October 14, 2026

1 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to SoloTrades ("we," "our," or "us"). We build tools specifically designed for independent contractors, tradespeople, freelancers, and service professionals to manage their jobs, create estimates, generate professional invoices, collect client signatures, and manage customer records.

Your privacy and trust are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy describes how SoloTrades collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

Privacy by Design: SoloTrades operates primarily with a local-first architecture. Your clients' contact details, job descriptions, estimates, invoices, and photos remain on your personal mobile device by default unless you choose to synchronize with secure cloud services.

2 Information We Collect & How We Obtain It

Depending on how you use SoloTrades, we may handle the following categories of information:

A. Business & Operational Data (User-Provided)

  • Company Profile: Business name, logo, phone number, email address, physical address, and tax/VAT details used on your invoice templates.
  • Customer & Client Records: Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, and service addresses entered to generate estimates and invoices or navigate to job sites.
  • Job & Financial Records: Service descriptions, scheduled dates, pricing items, estimate details, invoices, payment status records, and notes.
  • Digital Signatures: Signature strokes provided by you or your customers to authorize estimates and invoices directly on your screen.

B. Photos & Device Files

When you capture or upload photos for job documentation, receipts, or company logo branding, the app accesses your device camera and gallery only with your explicit runtime permission.

C. Device & Technical Information

To ensure app stability, deliver relevant ads, and verify subscription status, standard diagnostic information may be processed automatically, including:

  • Device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Anonymous Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) for ad delivery and analytics.
  • Crash logs and performance metrics to identify and fix technical glitches.

3 Device Permissions Used & Why

SoloTrades requests minimal Android permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify in-app purchases, display advertisements via Google AdMob, and synchronize cloud backup if enabled.
  • CAMERA & READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Storage): Required only if you decide to take photos on job sites, upload receipts, or customize your invoices with your company logo.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to provide reminders for upcoming scheduled jobs, overdue invoice notices, and calendar alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in device settings.

5 Data Storage, Retention, and Security

We employ robust technical and organizational safeguards to ensure your business data is treated confidentially:

  • Local Storage: Your primary customer database, jobs, quotes, and invoice files reside in sandboxed local device storage accessible only by the SoloTrades application.
  • Data in Transit: Any communications with third-party APIs (such as subscription status or cloud backup) are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • No Data Brokering: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your clients' personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

6 Your Rights & Data Control

You retain full control over your data within SoloTrades:

  • Access & Modification: You can edit any customer, invoice, job, or estimate at any time directly through the app.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete any individual client, job, estimate, or invoice using the built-in delete functions. Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage immediately erases all locally stored database records from your phone.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: You may reset or opt-out of personalized advertisements at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

7 Children's Privacy

SoloTrades is a business management tool intended for commercial and professional use by contractors, tradespeople, and working adults. We do not knowingly target, market to, or collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Economic Area).
